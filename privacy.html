Privacy Policy

What you write stays yours. No ads, nothing sold, no tracking across other apps.

Last updated November 10, 2026

On your phone

Your memos, lists, photos, drawings and voice memos are saved on this phone. No account needed.

If you sign in

Signing in with Apple or Google backs up your walls, tasks, photos, drawings and voice recordings to our backup provider, Supabase. We also keep the email associated with your account. Sign out to stop future backups. Suggestions in Ideas and readings in the guided modes need an account.

AI suggestions

Ideas can offer suggestions, and some guided modes can read back what you wrote. These use AI, and the app asks your permission before the first time anything is sent. When you use them, the memos you’re working on in that session (words, pictures and drawings), or what you wrote in that mode, go through our server to Google’s Gemini API, which writes the reply. Nothing else in the app goes, and never your name or email.

Google doesn’t use what we send through its paid API to train its models. Our server doesn’t keep your memos after the reply comes back. It keeps only a short record of each request (when it was made, which feature, and how it went) to apply fair-use limits. Records older than 35 days are cleared as new ones come in, and deleting your account deletes them all. Turn suggestions off anytime in Settings → AI in Ideas.

Pro and purchases

Pro is bought through your Apple ID, and Apple handles the payment. We never see your card. RevenueCat, our purchase provider, records which subscription is active and when it renews, linked to your account if you’re signed in. Our server asks RevenueCat whether an account has Pro, so Pro works on all your phones.

Usage data

To learn what’s working and where people get stuck, the app sends usage data to PostHog, our analytics provider, stored in the US. That means things like “a memo was made”, “an Ideas session started” or “the Pro page was opened”, which screens were opened, the kind of AI card that arrived, your device model, iOS and app version, and a random ID. Once you sign in, your account ID (never your email) is attached, so we can follow how the app gets used over time.

It can also record how a session moves around the screen: taps, scrolling and which screens. Every word and picture is hidden in these recordings, and drawings aren’t captured.

Usage data never includes what you write, draw, say or photograph, or the names of your projects. It isn’t used for ads or shared to track you. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland the app collects no usage data at all. Elsewhere, usage data is part of how the app works and there’s no switch for it in the app. If you’d like what’s been collected about you deleted, email kmworkshq@gmail.com.

Deleting your account

Settings, under Account: Delete account. Your account, the backup and anything stored with it are deleted for good. What’s on this phone is yours and stays. To have usage data linked to your account deleted as well, email kmworkshq@gmail.com.

Image search

When you search for a picture to add to your wall, the words you type are sent through our server to Brave Search, which finds matching images. Nothing else about you or your walls goes with them. A picture you add is saved like any other photo, with where it came from written on it.

Feedback

Now and then the app asks how it’s going. If you tell us why in the box it offers, your stars, what you wrote and the app’s version are emailed to our support inbox through Resend. Nothing else about you or your walls goes with them. You don’t have to answer.

Microphone and photos

Used only when you record or pick a photo. Voice memos are transcribed by Apple’s speech recognition, on your phone when it’s supported.

Changes

When the app changes what it collects, this page changes first, and the date at the top says when.

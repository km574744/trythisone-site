for creative minds & thought

A creative extension for anyone doing thoughtful work. Get what's in your head out of it, put it somewhere you can see all of at once, and come back to it when you're ready.

Three tabs, and that's the whole app

One for getting it out, one for what you've decided to do, and one for the days when neither of those is happening.

brain dump

An infinite wall you can push things around on

Write a note and it lands on the wall. Drag it, pinch the whole board, draw on a memo, record one out loud. Nothing is filed and nothing is sorted until you want it to be.

The notes have weight — drop one on top of another and it makes room for itself. Pile them up, spread them out, leave them where they fell.

tasks

Three shelves, not one list

Everything you've decided to do sits on one of three shelves, sorted by how far away it is rather than by how important someone says it is.

  • now
  • later
  • eventually

A task can carry a time, and the shelf shades with the clock — the morning end pale, the evening end deep — so a glance tells you where in the day you are.

reflect

Walkthroughs, one step at a time

Each one is a method that would work on a sheet of paper with a pen and nothing else. The app only holds the steps and keeps the pace.

A mind dump for when there's too much at once. A walkthrough for when you know the task and you're doing everything except it. What you write ends up back on the wall, and the ones worth doing become tasks.

No streaks. No counts. No notifications asking where you've been.

The app doesn't keep score, and it won't tell you that you've missed a day. It's there when you open it.

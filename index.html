The core of try this one

Nothing stays stuck in your head fighting for space. It gets out, it gets seen, and it stops weighing on you the moment it’s in front of you instead of behind your eyes.

Write down anything on your mind, at any time, and it becomes a memo. Move your memos anywhere on the canvas and lay out all your thoughts, all in front of your eyes.

Five ways to write something down

Anytime you write something down, you choose what it becomes.

  • Memo

    A quick note, written the moment a thought shows up.

  • Objects

    Text, arrows and drawing, for thoughts that aren’t just words.

  • Image

    A photo from your library, or a drawing, right where it’s relevant.

  • List

    A running set of items, kept together as one.

  • Voice memo

    A thought spoken instead of typed, for whenever you need it.

projects

A wall for every project

Gather your memos into projects of their own, each with its own wall, and take a brand new set of thoughts and ideas further.

The moment your mind is out in the open, you can finally see everything you’re carrying — and everything you’re capable of.

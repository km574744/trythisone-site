Support

Something not working, or not behaving the way you expected? Write in — it's a small operation and the mail comes straight to a person.

What happens to what I write?

Your memos, tasks and recordings are stored on your device. The app works fully without an account.

If you sign in, your content syncs so it's there on your other devices. If you use suggestions in Ideas or a mode that reads back what you wrote, what you're working on is sent to an AI to answer, only after you've allowed it. The app also sends usage data, never your words, except in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, where it collects none. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used to advertise to you.

The full details are in the privacy policy.

How does the wall work?

Tap to make a note and it lands on the wall. Drag it anywhere, pinch to zoom the whole board out, and drag on the background to pan around — the wall doesn't have edges.

A note can hold typed text, a drawing, or a voice recording. Nothing you write is filed away or sorted automatically; the wall stays how you left it.

How do tasks work?

Any memo can become a task, and it stays connected to the thought behind it. Open Tasks from the wall to see them all and tick them off.

Give a task a time and it'll remind you. Leave the time off and it just waits, which is the point.

Does it cost anything?

The app is free, with one Ideas session with suggestions a day. Pro is an optional subscription, weekly, monthly or yearly (monthly and yearly start with a free trial), that removes that limit and unlocks every wall pattern, pin and color set.

To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Anything already on your wall stays yours either way.

Can I delete my account and everything in it?

Yes — in the app, under Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account, the backup and anything stored with it are deleted for good. It can't be undone.

What's on this phone is yours and stays: deleting the account doesn't wipe the memos already on the device. Delete the app if you want those gone too.

If you'd rather it was done for you, or something's gone wrong with it, write to the address below from the email you signed up with.

I found a bug. What's useful to send?

Your iPhone model and iOS version, what you were doing just before it happened, and whether it happens every time. A screenshot or a screen recording is worth more than all of that put together.

Is there an iPad or Mac version?

Not yet — it's an iPhone app for now. If that's what you're waiting for, say so in an email; it's the kind of thing that decides what gets built next.

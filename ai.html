AI Use & Disclosure

Your mind is meant for creating.

Last updated November 10, 2026

Where AI is used

In two places, and only once you’ve allowed it: suggestions in an Ideas session, and the readings some guided modes give back about what you wrote. Everything else in the app (the wall, tasks, lists, drawing and search) works without AI.

Which AI, and what it sees

Google’s Gemini, through our own server. It sees the memos in the session you’re working on (words, pictures and drawings), or what you wrote in that mode. It doesn’t see the rest of your wall, your name or your email. See the Privacy Policy for the details.

What’s yours stays yours

Suggestions arrive as their own cards, drawn differently from your memos. Nothing the AI writes goes onto your wall as your own unless you choose to keep it.

Not training data

Nothing you write, draw or record is used to train a model, ours or anyone else’s. Google doesn’t train on what we send through its paid API.

Turning it off

Settings → AI in Ideas → Off. With it off, nothing is sent.

Voice memos

Transcribed with Apple’s speech recognition, which turns what you said into text without adding to it. It runs on the phone when the phone can do it. Where it can’t (some older devices and some languages), the audio goes to Apple to be transcribed.
